KNOW YOUR BATTLEFIELD

CONQUEST / COMBINED ARMS

Capture A: North Relay, B: Sawmill, and C: River Crossing. Stay within a sector's circle to secure it. Holding the majority drains enemy reinforcements. Reach zero enemy tickets to win.

W A S D Move / driveMOUSE Look / aimLMB Fire weaponRMB Precision aimSHIFT Boost / sprintF Enter / exit vehicleR Resupply / reloadQ Squad ordersSPACE Brake / jumpM Tactical mapESC Pause / settingsP Inside / outside view1 2 3 Order sector A / B / C

Your three squadmates can follow you, hold position, or attack a sector. Friendly sectors repair vehicles and replenish ammunition. Shells do splash damage; rear armor is weaker. Fallen combatants rejoin the fight after a short delay.

Aircraft: the AH-6 helicopter climbs with SHIFT and descends with SPACE, W/S moves it forward and back, A/D swings the nose. The B-24 bomber flies on real lift and gravity — SHIFT/SPACE is throttle, W/S is the elevator, A/D rolls into a bank, and it carries only three bombs. Every other weapon has unlimited ammunition; bombs reload inside a friendly sector.

Choose Karst Valley, Frost Reach, or Dune Sea before deployment. Karst grass bends in wind and parts around troops and vehicles. Press P for a crew-eye interior or the outside camera. Sentinel missile emplacements are marked AA on the tactical map: dismount, walk up, and press F to take the gunner seat. Aim at a hostile helicopter or bomber until the seeker locks, then fire. Missiles need line of sight and cannot lock onto ground units.

Click the battlefield to capture your mouse. Press Escape to release it. On a touchscreen, use the left stick to move and drag the right side to aim.
